# Code of conduct

Welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Ask questions in good faith, credit others, and critique the work respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer. If the concern involves that maintainer, contact another WHIMC maintainer or organization owner. Include only the information needed to review the incident. Maintainers should limit disclosure, hear from the people involved, and respond proportionately. They may remove content, warn a participant, or restrict participation. Retaliation for a good-faith report is unacceptable.
